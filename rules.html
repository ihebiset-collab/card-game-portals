Official Rule System

Rules of Play

A real-life battle of HP, pressure, truth, chaos, reputation, and Legacy. Quest cards stay outside the battle as personal life-level-up gifts.

Identity Setup Win & Lose Turns Categories Legacy Duels Quest Gifts

1. Game Identity

Each player is a Champion. The match is won by surviving pressure, breaking rivals, earning Legacy titles, and claiming the Crown.

Main idea: Action cards create battle, Emotion cards create pressure, Portal cards reveal truths, Chaos cards break rules, Legacy cards decide victory, and Quest cards help players level up in real life after the match.

2. Setup

Players

Best with 3-6 players. Playable with 2-8. More players are possible if each Champion has their own pack.

Starting Stats

Each Champion starts with 10 HP, 5 cards in hand, 0 unlocked Legacy cards, and 0 Broken Marks.

Battle Deck

Shuffle Action, Emotion, Chaos, and Portal cards together. This is the deck used during the match.

Legacy Set

Keep each player's Legacy cards outside the deck, locked and visible near that player.

Quest Deck

Quest cards are not used during battle. They are drawn after the match as personal gifts.

First Player

Choose randomly. Play continues clockwise unless a card reverses the direction.

If a deck runs out

Shuffle that player's discard pile to create a new Battle Deck.

3. How to Win and Lose

Main Win: Crown Victory

Unlock 3 Legacy cards, claim The Crown of Legacy, then survive one full round until the start of your next turn.

Combat Win: Fallen Victory

You win if every other player becomes Fallen.

Broken State

When your HP reaches 0, you do not lose immediately. You enter Broken State.

  • Gain 1 Broken Mark.
  • Discard 1 card.
  • Lose 1 unlocked Legacy card if you have one.
  • Return to 3 HP.
  • Your next turn becomes a Recovery Turn.

Fallen State

If you receive 2 Broken Marks, you become Fallen and lose the match. Fallen players may still vote in Portal, Judgment, and Duel moments, but they cannot win.

4. Turn Structure

  1. Draw 1 card.
  2. Play 1 card OR perform 1 Basic Action.
  3. Resolve all effects.
  4. Unlock any Legacy card you earned.
  5. End turn. Max hand size is 7 cards.

Basic Actions

Basic Strike

Deal 1 damage to one player.

Focus

Draw 1 card.

Recover

Gain 1 HP.

Guard

Reduce the next damage you take by 1.

Recovery Turn

Draw 1 card. You cannot attack and cannot play a Portal. You may only defend if attacked. Then your turn ends.

5. Card Categories

Action

Battle power: damage, defense, HP steal, hand control, skipped turns, and reversed turn order.

Emotion

Social pressure: the target performs or answers. Refuse, dodge, or fake it -> lose 1 HP.

Chaos

Rule breaking: cancel, reverse, duplicate, swap, or force random effects.

Portal

QR mystery: choose a player, scan the gate, open the page, and obey the result.

Legacy

Reputation and victory: earned titles that unlock effects and lead to the Crown.

Quest

Personal growth gifts: not used in battle. Drawn after the match.

Portal Rule

When a Portal is played, choose a player, scan that Portal's QR code, press Open the Gate, and obey the result.

  • If the player completes the prompt honestly, they may draw 1 card OR recover 1 HP.
  • If the player refuses, dodges, or gives a fake answer, they lose 1 HP OR discard 1 card.
  • Gate of Destiny is harsher: refusing costs 2 HP OR 2 cards.

Emotion Rule

If the target completes the Emotion prompt, the effect ends normally. If the table agrees the performance was legendary, the target may draw 1 card.

Chaos Rule

Chaos cards override normal rules unless another Chaos card or a direct cancel effect stops them.

6. Legacy Rules

Legacy cards are not drawn. They start locked. When a player meets a Legacy card's Gain condition, they unlock it and place it in front of them.

Only 1 Legacy may be unlocked per turn Legacy cards can be challenged The Crown must survive one full round

Each Legacy effect may be used once per time you unlock or gain that Legacy. If the Legacy is lost and gained again later, its effect resets.

Alpha Crest

Gain: Break 2 players. Effect: Command 1 Portal answer. Lose: Refuse a challenge.

Saint's Mark

Gain: Protect 1 player. Effect: Block 1 betrayal. Lose: Betray an ally.

The Challenger's Medal

Gain: Win 2 duels. Effect: Challenge 1 Legacy. Lose: Lose a duel.

The Scholar's Seal

Gain: Win a truth vote. Effect: Force a deeper answer. Lose: Fake an answer.

The Phoenix Emblem

Gain: Return from Broken State. Effect: Ignore 1 loss. Lose: Give up after breaking.

The Crown of Legacy

Gain: Hold 3 Legacies. Effect: Only Legacies may challenge you. Lose: Lose a Crown duel.

7. Duels and Legacy Challenges

Duel Procedure

  1. The challenger declares the duel.
  2. Both players open Gate of Judgment or Gate of Courage.
  3. Each player answers one prompt.
  4. The table votes who answered better.
  5. The winner wins the duel.

If there are only 2 players, answer the prompts first, then use best-of-3 rock-paper-scissors if a vote is impossible.

Duel Reward

Winner

Draws 1 card.

Loser

Loses 1 HP.

Legacy Challenge Cost

To challenge a Legacy, use The Challenger's Medal, discard 2 cards, or lose 2 HP.

If Challenger Wins

They take the challenged Legacy card.

If Defender Wins

They keep the Legacy card. Challenger loses 1 HP.

8. Quest Cards: Life Gift

Quest cards are not part of the battle. They are personal life-level-up missions drawn after the match.

Every Player

Draws 1 Quest card after the match.

Winner

Draws 2 Quest cards, keeps 1, and may gift the other to any player.

Quest cards are about discipline, fitness, reflection, spirituality, gratitude, habits, and personal growth.
