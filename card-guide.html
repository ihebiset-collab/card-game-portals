Action Cards

Action cards are the battle system. They control HP, attacks, defense, hand control, skipped turns, and turn order.

Deal Damage
Choose 1 player. Deal 2 damage.
Reflect Damage
Reaction: send incoming damage back to the attacker.
Disarm
Choose 1 player. They cannot play an Attack card on their next turn.
Force Card Drop
Choose 1 player. They discard 1 random card.
Block Attack
Reaction: cancel 1 incoming attack against you.
Cancel Turns
Reaction: cancel 1 turn-control effect such as Skip, Reverse, or Cancel.
Skip Player
Choose 1 player. They skip their next turn.
Steal HP
Choose 1 player. They lose 2 HP and you recover 2 HP.
Swap Hands
Choose 1 player. Swap your entire hand with theirs.
Reverse Turns
Reverse the direction of play immediately.
Defense cards can be played as reactions. If multiple reactions happen, the newest card resolves first.

Emotion Cards

Emotion cards are social pressure cards. They create dares, tricks, taunts, reactions, and table tension.

Confession Time
Target must confess or answer honestly. Refuse or fake it -> lose 1 HP.
Dance or Draw
Target must perform the card's dare. If the table rejects it, lose 1 HP.
Dramatic Entry
Target performs a dramatic entrance or speech with full energy.
Echo Game
Target echoes or mirrors another player's words, tone, or energy as instructed.
Face Freeze
Target must freeze their face and resist reacting until the prompt ends.
Mock Crown
Target receives a mocking title or taunt and must respond without dodging.
Silent Roaster
Target must roast or be roasted under the card's silent/limited rule.
Vibe Switch
Target must switch attitude, vibe, or behavior as instructed.
Voice Swap
Target must speak in the assigned voice until the effect ends.
Who Smirked
Exposes reactions. The player caught reacting faces the card's penalty.
If the table agrees the performance was legendary, the target may draw 1 card.

Chaos Cards

Chaos cards break normal rules. They interrupt, cancel, duplicate, reverse, swap, or force unpredictable effects.

The Trickster's Hand
Swap 1 card with another player at random.
The Void Mirror
Reverse the effect of any card played this round.
The Eclipse Sigil
Cancel 1 card's effect this turn.
The Paradox Token
Duplicate a card's effect once, then lose this card.
The Wild Surge
Draw 1 random card from any category and activate it instantly if possible.
The Void Whisper
Draw 1 random card from any category. Obey it instantly. Refuse -> lose 2 HP.
If Wild Surge or Void Whisper draws a Quest during battle, ignore the Quest and draw again. Legacy only unlocks if its Gain condition is met.

Portal Cards

Portal cards are QR mystery cards. They create intense truths, player choices, social tension, and random fate.

Gate of Truth
Hot truths, hidden opinions, and direct exposure.
Gate of Bonds
Trust, loyalty, friendship tension, and emotional connection.
Gate of Shadows
Hidden weakness, insecurity, masks, darker thoughts, and reflection.
Gate of Desire
Attention, jealousy, attraction, ambition, ego, and secret wants.
Gate of Judgment
Group voting, reputation checks, public pressure, and worthiness.
Gate of Betrayal
Loyalty tests, sacrifice choices, trust breaks, and betrayal pressure.
Gate of Destiny
Random effects, rewards, punishments, truths, choices, and chaotic fate.
Gate of Courage
Bold confessions, direct confrontation, brave honesty, and pressure.
Complete honestly -> draw 1 card OR recover 1 HP. Refuse/dodge/fake -> lose 1 HP OR discard 1 card. Gate of Destiny refusal costs 2 HP OR 2 cards.

Legacy Cards

Legacy cards are earned titles. They are the main victory path and represent reputation, power, survival, and status.

Alpha Crest
Gain: Break 2 players. Effect: Command 1 Portal answer. Lose: Refuse a challenge.
Saint's Mark
Gain: Protect 1 player. Effect: Block 1 betrayal. Lose: Betray an ally.
The Challenger's Medal
Gain: Win 2 duels. Effect: Challenge 1 Legacy. Lose: Lose a duel.
The Scholar's Seal
Gain: Win a truth vote. Effect: Force a deeper answer. Lose: Fake an answer.
The Phoenix Emblem
Gain: Return from Broken State. Effect: Ignore 1 loss. Lose: Give up after breaking.
The Crown of Legacy
Gain: Hold 3 Legacies. Effect: Only Legacies may challenge you. Lose: Lose a Crown duel.
Win path: unlock 3 Legacies, claim the Crown, and survive one full round.

Quest Cards

Quest cards are not battle cards. They are personal life-level-up gifts after the game ends.

Power Start
Morning routine: make your bed or clean your place, stretch, freshen up, and complete hygiene.
Sacred Reps
Fitness mission: push-ups, shadow boxing, plank, and sit-ups.
Prayer Signal
Spiritual mission: pray, make a wish, give thanks, and ask forgiveness.
Read to Grow
Learning mission: read, write the best line, and write one lesson learned.
Small Step
Choose one goal, write the first action, take the action now, and reflect.
Schedule Ritual
Create a real schedule for a new habit and commit to it.
Personal Creed
Define values, principles, standards, dislikes, and what you want to change.
Gratitude & Vision
Practice gratitude, visualize your dream achieved, and reflect on your direction.
After the match: every player draws 1 Quest. Winner draws 2, keeps 1, and may gift the other.
